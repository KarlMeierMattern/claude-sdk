# One-Day Meal Plan for Marathon Training

Here is a one-day meal plan for an athlete aiming to optimize performance for marathon training with high-intensity endurance focus:

* **Calorie Target:** Approximately 3100 calories
* **Macronutrient Breakdown:** Protein (135g), Fat (85g), Carbs (430g)

## Meal Plan:

* **Breakfast (7:00 AM):** Oatmeal (100g dry weight) with banana (150g), honey (25g), and whole milk (250g). 2 eggs scrambled (100g).
    * Protein: 25g, Fat: 15g, Carbs: 85g

* **Mid-Morning Snack (10:00 AM):** Bagel (85g) with peanut butter (30g) and jam (20g). Orange juice (250ml).
    * Protein: 12g, Fat: 10g, Carbs: 75g

* **Lunch (1:00 PM):** Grilled chicken breast (150g) with brown rice (120g dry weight), steamed broccoli (200g), and olive oil (15ml for cooking).
    * Protein: 45g, Fat: 15g, Carbs: 95g

* **Pre-Workout Snack (3:30 PM):** Energy bar (65g) with sports drink (500ml).
    * Protein: 10g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 85g

* **Dinner (7:00 PM):** Lean ground turkey (160g) pasta (120g dry weight) with marinara sauce (150g), olive oil (10ml), and parmesan cheese (15g).
    * Protein: 38g, Fat: 20g, Carbs: 95g

* **Evening Snack (9:30 PM):** Greek yogurt (200g) with granola (40g) and berries (100g).
    * Protein: 15g, Fat: 7g, Carbs: 50g

This meal plan emphasizes complex carbohydrates and lean proteins strategically distributed throughout the day to fuel intense training sessions, support muscle recovery, and maintain sustained energy for endurance performance.
